# Packing benchmark — 2026-10-21

Cases: 278 (fixtures 60, demo orders 18, random orders 200, seed 20261022).
Demo orders 1006 and 1017 are left out: their products have no known size even in the ground truth.
Same units, cartons and prices for every engine. Layouts judged by the Rassah validator, cost by one cost model (carton + dimensional-weight shipping, SAR).

## Sizes missing from the store

- Both vendor APIs require length, width, height and weight for every item in the request. With no sizes entered by the merchant they can pack 0/20 demo orders.
- Rassah reads sizes from the product page (15 products state them, 12 are estimated with a safety margin, 3 need measuring): 18/20 demo orders pack with no data entry, the rest after one measurement per product.
